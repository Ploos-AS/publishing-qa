# Reviewer roles

## OpenAI — Technical Editor / QA Lead

Focus on technical correctness, code and code explanations, internal consistency, exercises and solutions, and prerequisite ordering.

## Anthropic — Pedagogical & Manuscript Editor

Focus on readability, pedagogical progression, clarity, manuscript flow, and missing explanations.

## Google — Fact & Consistency Reviewer

Focus on factual cross-checking, terminology consistency, chapter-to-chapter contradictions, and NB/EN semantic parity.

## Mistral — Independent Reviewer

Focus on independent language and structure review, technical second opinion, and European-language perspective.

## Independence rule

First-pass reviewers must not see the other reviewers' findings.

## Judge role

A separate judge stage may normalize, deduplicate and group findings after all first-pass reviews have completed. The judge must not convert model agreement into factual certainty.
